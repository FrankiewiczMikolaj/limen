The core loop works: jobs, worktrees, steers, finish and review carried 273 jobs. The losses come from the harness under load and from one assumption that no longer holds: one coordinator per plant. Alice ran up to 19 coordinators at once, started and shepherded by Tony. The models held rules and negotiated well; Limen's records, timeouts and landing did not keep up.

Tweak

  1. Wake records tell the truth and do not resend
  2. Spawn returns the job ID in seconds
  3. A detached job hears its deadline before the timeout

Build

  1. Seat admission: a cap on running jobs, with a queue
  2. One landing path that records proof
  3. Starting a coordinator is one Limen command

Remove

  1. Remove the group runtime; keep teams as prose
  2. Drop the per-edit styleguide reminder
  3. Move rare recipes out of the always-on shop manual

Lens

Each item is judged on what models do well today, what they still cannot do, and what stays true when they get better.

Native now

Opus 5.5 coordinators kept every standing rule for 30 h at 600k+ tokens, with no slower replies. Coordinators split file ownership in plain text (about 41 peer messages, 0 lost). Workers found the real cause in 4 of 4 briefs that guessed wrong. Steers were obeyed within seconds.

Still a limit

A model has no wall clock: 270 reviewer-minutes ended with no verdict at the 90-minute limit. A model's "landed" is a claim, not proof (F1047). Models retry a refused shared resource (64 lease attempts in one job). A hand-written mask leaked three secret values.

Fast takeoff

Remove what compensates for weak instruction-following or weak coordination: those weaknesses did not show. Build what stays true at any model strength: deadlines, finite machines, proof of landing, ownership records, and one place for Adam's decisions.

Tweak

Small changes to behavior that exists. Top 5 first.

1Wake records tell the truth and do not resend

Evidence
49 of 240 job wakes reached the coordinator twice, 0–3 min apart. 21 jobs logged "automatic delivery stopped after 2 unsuccessful attempts"; 20 of them were in fact delivered, twice. Causes in the logs: herdr exceeded 15000ms (37), timed out waiting for agent status (13), submitted; no turn observed (22). Tony then told a coordinator "The limen wake did not reach you"; the session shows it had arrived 40 s earlier.
Why
Under load the Herdr CLI times out after it has delivered the text, and OMP queues text into a busy turn, so "no turn observed" is normal. The record reads a slow CLI as a lost wake. Models handle a queued message well; only the harness guesses wrong.
Effect
Check the pane before any resend; record "delivered, turn not seen"; never resend the same text. No duplicate turns; a record bots and people can trust.

Size 1 slice, 1–2 hConfidence high

2Spawn returns the job ID in seconds

Evidence
Timed spawn calls in coordinators: median 58 s, p75 5 min, p90 10 min, max 19 min. Detached and review spawns: median about 3 min. 16 logs show prepare failed: spawnSync /bin/sh ETIMEDOUT (300 s cap). A 200 s shell limit killed one spawn mid-setup (exit 143). Worktree add, prune of finished worktrees, and --prepare all run before the ID prints (src/commands/spawn.ts:437–443).
Why
The vision says spawn returns the ID in seconds and hands the job to a supervisor. Under load the synchronous path breaks that promise and can leave a job the caller cannot name.
Effect
Print the ID right after publication; run prune and prepare in the supervisor and log the result. Coordinators stop blocking for minutes.

Size 1 slice, 2–3 hConfidence medium-high

3A detached job hears its deadline before the timeout

Evidence
3 detached reviews ran the full 90 min with no verdict: 270 reviewer-minutes, 70–77 min of each spent in waits on builds and the GUI lease. No reviewer mentions its limit; templates/reviewer.md has no time wording. Coordinators sent time steers by hand ("wrap up now", "You hit the time limit. Do NOT run new model calls"). limen continue --timeout is refused ("unknown continue option").
Why
A model cannot see the clock. This does not fade as models improve; only the harness knows the deadline.
Effect
At 80% of the timeout, one steer through the existing hook: "N min left: write the verdict or commit now; mark unrun checks unverified." A timeout ends with a verdict or a commit.

Size small slice, about 1 hConfidence high

4Detached is the default when nobody types in the tab

Evidence
141 hosted jobs; 0 messages typed by a person into a worker tab (all in-tab text came from coordinators, steers or bots). Hosted cost: 49 "timed out waiting for agent startup" warnings, 13 hosted start failures, 12 "another tab took focus during hosted start". No timeout: hosted wall p90 5.4 h, max 11 h; Tony sent 21 nudges about long or silent work.
Why
The hosted tab exists so the owner can type. Adam works through Tony, so the tab costs without use. Detached keeps a visible log tab and adds a timeout, a tool cap and process containment.
Effect
Default to --detached with a board-set timeout (23% of hosted jobs ran over 90 active minutes, so not 90 min); --tab stays opt-in. Fewer start failures, no focus theft, bounded runs.

Size small slice + board lineConfidence medium (changes an owner default)

5Fix the CLI papercuts agents route around

Evidence
Node prints ExperimentalWarning: Type Stripping on every limen call: 90 of 481 limen calls add a grep filter, 68 tool results carry it. limen land asks for a TTY confirm; agents pass --yes every time after one refusal. continue refuses --timeout.
Why
Each workaround costs a token-heavy retry or a longer command, every time, in every session.
Effect
Silence the warning in bin/limen; skip the confirm when stdin is not a TTY; accept --timeout on continue.

Build

New capability that stays valuable as models improve. Top 5 first.

1Seat admission: a cap on running jobs, with a queue

Evidence
Load averages 80–320 on a 16-core Mac with 15 jobs running; git status took 18 s; 8 detached starts failed ("detached wrapper did not become ready"); one review spent 28 min on a prebuild; a lab check failed at 5.2 s against a 5 s window "under load". One worker made 64 GUI-lease attempts (63 refused); check batches re-ran 5–13 times. Tony's rule "at most 2 workers at a time" is per coordinator, and 19 coordinators ran at once.
Why
Machines stay finite as agent count grows. Prose limits per coordinator cannot add up across coordinators; only the seat sees the total.
Effect
A board-set max running: spawn records the job as queued and the supervisor starts it when a slot frees; an optional named lease (--needs gui) for one shared resource; status shows queue and load. Fewer timeouts and reruns.

Size 2 slices, 4–6 hConfidence medium-high

2One landing path that records proof

Evidence
Coordinators ran git merge 143 times and limen land 29 times; land hit 6 conflicts in the shared checkout, worked around with git update-ref 3 times. F1047's outcome said "landed" for a merge that never reached alice-v6; a drift audit found it 8 h later. Three coordinators would not push alice-v6 because it held another coordinator's unpushed commits. A landed F1044 slice still showed as a waiting candidate and caused a false nudge.
Why
A model's "landed" is a claim; the plant branch is the proof. With many coordinators on one branch, landing must be serialized and checkable at any model strength.
Effect
limen land takes a plant lock, resolves conflicts in an integration worktree (land --continue), pushes when the board says so, and records the merge SHA in the job record. Status flags any outcome that claims a landing the branch does not have.

Size 2 slices, 4–6 hConfidence high (need), medium (shape)

3Starting a coordinator is one Limen command

Evidence
Tony started 26 Alice coordinator sessions in 3 days by hand (open a tab, type "Read /tmp/tony-briefs/X.md and follow it fully"). The first turn failed in 26 of 26 Alice coordinator sessions ("No conversation found with session ID", pi-claude bridge); 24 needed a typed "Retry: do the task above now." 9 of 19 coordinator tabs have no handle; the briefs live in /tmp.
Why
Agents starting coordinators is the trend. The coordinator owns the work but has no record, while every job has one.
Effect
limen coordinator start --brief FILE --label L stores the brief under .limen/coordinators/, opens the tab, waits for ready, sends the brief, confirms a turn, retries once on a first-turn provider error, and records pane, label and starter. Status and wakes read that record.

Size 2 slices, 4–6 hConfidence medium-high

4One list of open owner decisions

Evidence
Tony asked coordinators to write their open questions into /tmp/tony-briefs/*-decisions.md, relayed answers by hand ("Adam's answers on F1074 (verbatim intent): Q1 YES…"), and once relayed a wrong one: "Adam confirms…", then "Adam has NOT confirmed" 35 s later. Tickets already carry a needs-adam field.
Why
Adam's attention is the scarce resource. One list read once beats questions spread over 19 panes and /tmp files.
Effect
limen status and a decision.open webhook event list every needs-adam ask in the plant with feature and question; an answer clears it.

Size 1 slice, 2–3 hConfidence medium

5Durable notes and claims between coordinators

Evidence
On 10-18, 8 coordinators exchanged about 41 messages with raw herdr pane send-text, pane run and agent prompt: 0 lost, but delays up to 4 min, one stuck in an input box, 11 commands backgrounded and 4 waits skipped by incoming text. Ownership deals lived in /tmp files and in ledger edits that were promised, not applied. One duplicate rename was caught by chance.
Why
Negotiation is native: the models did it well. What fails is the record: pane text and /tmp do not survive.
Effect
limen note <coordinator> "text" routed like a wake, with a receipt, under .limen/notes/; a plant claims file (path glob, owner, feature) that status shows and spawn checks for overlap.

Size 2 slices, 4–6 hConfidence medium

Remove

Scaffolding that compensates for weaknesses the sessions did not show. Top 5 first.

1Remove the group runtime; keep teams as prose

Evidence
0 limen group start in 3 days. Adam asked to "spin limen group of agents" (F1048); the coordinator found the command "can't express mixed team configurations" and ran 7 plain spawns with draft and critique rounds. Board cleanup (4 teams), F1042 (3 groups × 3 models) and F1048 all ran as plain spawns and delivered.
Why
Rounds and cross-reading are native model work. The runtime (src/commands/group.ts 663 lines, hook/group-peer.ts 259, two templates, docs/groups.md, about 400 words in every coordinator prompt) compensates for a weakness models did not show. The seat cap bounds fan-out better.
Effect
About 1,100 fewer lines, a shorter coordinator prompt, one way to run teams.

Size 1 slice + review, 2–3 hConfidence medium (Adam asked for groups by name)

2Drop the per-edit styleguide reminder

Evidence
Appended 3,662 times to code-edit results, failed edits included; a styleguide read followed within 5 calls 17 times (0.5%). The vision reminder: 499 appends, 7 reads. Both files already ride the system prompt on every call.
Why
Rule retention held for 30 h at 600k+ tokens. A heading list repeated inside tool results is data the model parses on every later call.
Effect
Remove the styleguide reminder; fire the vision reminder once per session. Cleaner tool results.

Size under 1 hConfidence medium (no behavior A/B)

3Move rare recipes out of the always-on shop manual

Evidence
templates/agents.md is 7,260 words and rides every coordinator call. Not used in 3 days: groups (396 words), GitHub doorbell (254), picture (256), keeper (182), personal routes (132), quality (104), workspace (91), Linear. Window uses: keeper 0, judge 0, quality 0, doorbell 0, Linear 0, diff 0, open 0.
Why
Models read a named doc on demand well. A long manual dilutes the rules that matter on every turn: spawn, wake, land, recovery.
Effect
One pointer line per recipe ("Teams: read docs/groups.md"); about 1,700 fewer words per coordinator call.

Size 1 slice, 1–2 hConfidence medium-high

4Stop hand-keeping NOW and PROVEN on the board

Evidence
F946, F947 and F948 landed on main, but the board has 0 lines for them and their folders are still in active/. Alice's board said S23c was running after its job had failed. Workers may not edit the board, and bot-driven coordinators skip the ritual.
Why
Lanes and job records already hold this state. A hand copy drifts; a generated view stays true.
Effect
Generate NOW and PROVEN from lanes and landed jobs; keep TRACK (owner decisions) by hand.

Size 1–2 slices, 3–4 hConfidence medium (the vision names the board coordinator-kept)

5Drop count rules that models ignore

Evidence
worker.md: "Ten reads without a changed file means make the probe edit." 82% of 148 Alice workers made more than 10 reads before their first edit (median 25; 39% over 30). No sign these jobs did worse.
Why
An ignored rule costs tokens and teaches that rules are optional. Orientation reading is cheap at 1M context.
Effect
Keep the outcome rules (commit the first coherent piece early, leave residue); remove the count.

Keep

  • Job records and Git as truth: every scout traced events from job files without gaps.
  • Worktree isolation: 273 jobs in 3 days, conflicts only at merge.
  • Steer: 114 delivered; obeyed within seconds in every traced case but one bound.
  • finish tool and the durable finish outbox (F946): Tony's shepherding depends on it.
  • Review where a mistake is expensive: 4 proven FAILs in 43 verdicts (a sync cut race, a 20 MP photo that breaks older replicas, an MCP scenario, a red test).

Not Limen

Engine and bridge faults seen in the window. Route them to OMP and the pi-claude bridge.

  • pi-claude bridge: first turn "No conversation found with session ID" in 26 of 26 Alice coordinators and 3 of 4 probes.
  • Codex lane: 24 "native turn lane cannot accept stateful WebSocket messages" errors (recovered).
  • OMP hashline edits: 237 edits anchored on lines never shown, 58 stale hashes; loops of 10–14 in one session.
  • Secrets: a coordinator's sed mask covered only TAURI_* lines and printed three values (APPLE_PASSWORD, APPLE_CERTIFICATE_PASSWORD, ELEVEN_LABS_API_KEY, 10-16 14:49 UTC); ps aux printed a Cursor key. No Limen surface carried them. Output redaction belongs in the engine.

Method and limits

  • Source: every OMP session file modified 2026-10-15 19:30 to 2026-10-18 19:30 UTC: 385 job sessions under <plant>/.limen/jobs/*/session, 33 coordinator and probe sessions and 11 subagent sessions under ~/.omp/agent/sessions. The 1.1 GB figure in the ask is the whole sessions folder; the 3-day slice is 504 MB.
  • Index: one Python pass for plant, role, model, duration, tools, errors, wakes, steers and inputs; job logs and notify records for wake and start outcomes. Seven read-only scouts traced chosen sessions; I checked their key claims in the files.
  • Durations exclude parent history copied into continue jobs. Active time caps gaps at 5 min.
  • Not read: Pi sessions (78 files, not OMP), Tony's and Johnny's own transcripts, Claude Code mirrors except to remove leaked values.
  • Adam's words reach OMP only through Tony and Johnny; every quote attributed to Adam is a relay.
  • One scout copied the three leaked values into this review's own transcripts; I replaced them with <redacted> in both files (24 places) and found no other copy.
  • Size is agent time for one worker plus review. Confidence is mine.
